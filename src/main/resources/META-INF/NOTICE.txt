Technomancy: Continuation

Original Technomancy by Democretes, theflogat and Mordenkainen.
Original source: https://github.com/Mordenkainen/Technomancy
Baseline: Technomancy 0.12.5 for Minecraft 1.7.10.
Original project: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/technomancy
The original project's declared license is Apache License 2.0.

This is a modified, independently implemented Forge 1.20.1 port by the
TC4Revival project (2026). The Java code, registry identifiers, persistence,
networking, recipes and rendering adapters have been changed for the modern
runtime. Model geometry was converted from the original author definitions
through Blockbench. The black-hole sphere and reservoir were rebuilt to match
the author's shapes. This port is not an official release by the original authors.

Original textures, audio and legacy localization are NOT included in this JAR.
They are supplied through Thaumcraft 4: Continuation's original-resource loader,
which retrieves and verifies the original 0.12.5 distribution for the player.
Minecraft, Forge, Thaumcraft, Botania, Blood Magic and JEI remain the works of
their respective authors and are not bundled with this product.
